The 14-day rule and the digital-content exception. For most distance purchases, you have 14 days from your purchase to cancel it without giving a reason. For digital content delivered immediately — like a key that's visible right after payment — a statutory exception to that right applies. That exception applies here because, at checkout, you expressly indicate that delivery may start immediately, and expressly acknowledge that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal once the key has been shown or emailed to you. That happens through a separate checkbox at checkout, apart from the ordinary terms checkbox — see the withdrawal waiver for the exact wording. If you don't tick that box, we can't complete your order: without that express consent, we're not allowed to deliver immediately.

A faulty key is reported through the contact form, with screenshots. We never automatically send a replacement key, and we don't refund before the supplier has responded — see the procedure in the terms and conditions.

Statutory conformity guarantee. Separately from the right of withdrawal above, and separately from the faulty-key procedure, you're covered as a consumer by the 2-year statutory guarantee for consumer goods (art. 1649bis et seq. of the old Belgian Civil Code) — which also applies to digital content and digital services. This guarantee doesn't lapse just because you lost your right of withdrawal through immediate delivery.
