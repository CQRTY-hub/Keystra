# Keystra — How to apply a top-up code

**Language:** English
**Version:** none tracked separately
**Status:** —

---

Top-up codes add in-game currency or points directly to your account — no separate wallet step.

**Steps:**

1. Launch the game and sign in to the account you want to top up.
2. Find the in-game "redeem code" or "top up" screen — usually in the store or settings menu.
3. Enter the code exactly as shown on your order confirmation or in your confirmation email.
4. The balance appears in your account immediately, ready to spend in-game.

**Notes:**

- Top-up codes are region-locked to the account region shown on the product page.
- Once revealed, a top-up code can't be returned — see the refund policy for what this means for the 14-day withdrawal right.
